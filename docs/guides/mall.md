# The Abelaa Mall

Abelaa Mall is the place where you will find only shops; it's Abelaa's hypermarket.<br>
To access Abelaa Mall, you must click on ***Abelaa Mall*** in the menu
from any of the app's features .

## **What is the purpose of the Abelaa Mall?**

Abelaa Mall allows you to have all your favorite shops and everything else in one place. If you have a clear idea of ​​what you're looking for or who you're looking for, there's no need **to wander around the marketplace** .

## **How do I get to Abelaa Mall?**

To access **Abelaa Mall**, from the application's home page, click on **MarketPlace** and then on ***Abelaa Mall*** in the menu.

Once inside the **Abelaa Mall** you have the option to:

- To buy and/or trade an item
- To search for a shop
- Browse all the shops in Abelaa

## **How do I find a shop in Abelaa Mall?**

The **Listing Search** feature allows you to quickly find a specific store available in Abelaa Mall. Using the search bar, you can easily identify the stores most relevant to you.

### ***Access the Abelaa Mall listings***

To view store listings:

1. Open the Marketplace section in the application.
2. Select an Inventory Group .
3. The listings available for this group are displayed automatically.

Each group contains the listings published by its members or administrators.

### *Search by keyword*

The search function allows you to search for an item to buy using a keyword in a store. Perform a keyword search in Abelaa Mall.

1. Click on the Search bar.
2. Enter the word you are looking for in the search field.

The list of matching products will be displayed at the bottom.
