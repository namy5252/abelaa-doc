# The Book Exchange

The **Book Exchange** allows you to exchange your books with other users in a simple and smart way.

The system:

- Analyzes the books you **offer**
- Compares them with the books you **want**
- And finds **compatible and fair** exchanges

## **How ​​book exchange works**

The exchange takes place in **3 simple steps**:

- Choose the books you offer
- Choose the books you want
- Start the search for matches

## **Step 1 – Books to exchange**

1. Go to **Dashboard → Book Exchange**

2. In the "**Books to exchange**" section:

    - Search your personal inventory

3. Click **Add** to select a book

> Important:
> - You can remove a book at any time
> - Multiple books can be selected
> - Your choices are saved automatically

## **Step 2 – Books to find**

1. In the "**Books wanted**" section:

    - Search among the books offered by other users

2. Each book displays:

    - Image
    - Title
    - Author

3. Click **Add** to add it to your list

> Important:
> - The search is instantaneous
> - Selected books remain visible

## **Step 3 – Finding Matches**

1. When you have:

    - At least one book offered
    - And one book you are looking for

2. Click on "**Find Matches**".

3. The system:

    - Compares your books with those of other users
    - Checks the compatibility of the exchanges
    - Suggests the best available results

### ***Exchange Results***

The results are displayed **by book offered**.

For each book, you can see up to ***3 possible trades***, including:

- 📖 Title and author
- 📍 Seller location
- 📦 Quantity available
- 🔁 Type of trade
- 📞 WhatsApp contact

### ***Helpful Tips***

- Accurate titles and authors improve results
- The more books you offer, the better your chances
- Check back regularly for new offers
