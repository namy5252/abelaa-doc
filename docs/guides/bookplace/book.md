# Book Section

The book section is Abelaa's bookstore. Here you can find and buy a wide variety of books.

## Accessing the Book Marketplace

The book marketplace is where all the books available on Abelaa are listed. From here you can:

- View available books
- Search for a book or a category of books
- Buy a book
- Trade a book

To access the book marketplace, simply go to the application's home page and click on the **BookPlace** tab. You will then see the list of available books and their prices.

The library is where all your books are listed. You can fill and empty it as you wish to track the value of your books and effectively manage your possessions.

## How do I see all my books?

To view your books in the library, go to the home page and tap "Bookplace," then "My Library."

In this library, you can see all the books you have saved. If the list is empty, it simply means you haven't added any.

> If you have already saved books, but they are not appearing in your library, [contact us](https://wa.me/+237698778055)

### *Access the book list*

To view your library as a list, simply click the "Open book list" button on the library page. From there, you can: [search for a book](#find-a-book-in-the-library), [edit a book](#edit-a-book), and [export your book list](#export-your-book-list).

## How to add a book to your library?

You can save the books you have so you know exactly what you own.

You can then choose to give them to an individual, a loved one, or even a company, or to sell them.

To add a new book to your library, simply go to the library page and:

1. Click on "Add a new book"
2. Enter the book information
3. Add a main image (required) and one or more secondary image(s) (optional)
4. Confirm to save using the ***Add*** button

> Note: If you feel the confirmation button isn't working, try scrolling back up the form and check that all the required fields (marked with a red *) are filled in.

## How to find a book in the library?

In your library, you can search for a book. You can do this using the search bar in the library list or directly on the library page.

### *Search by Keyword*

The search function allows you to find one of your books using a keyword. To perform a keyword search in the library or from the book list, you must:

1. Click on the Search bar.
2. Enter the word you are looking for.
3. The book will be displayed at the bottom.

### *Add a Filter*

Adding a filter allows you to search for one or more books with common attributes. Filters are located to the right of the search bar. You can apply one or more filters by selecting their values.

Note: If you cannot find your book, make sure you have entered it correctly or that you haven't already deleted it from the library. If it still doesn't appear, please contact [Customer Service](https://wa.me/+237698778055)

## How to Edit Book Information?

You can edit a book at any time after adding it to your library, and it will be instantly updated in your library and on BookPlace if you have already listed it for sale there.

## How do I remove a book from my library?

In the library, hover over the book you want to remove.

Click the three vertical dots, select the "Delete book" option, and then confirm the removal.

> Note: Deleting a book is irreversible. Abelaa cannot recover books you have deleted. You can only recover deleted books by manually adding them back to your library. Please note that if the book was listed in BookPlace, it will not be removed even if you have already sold or exchanged it.
