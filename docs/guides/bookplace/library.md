# My Library

In the My Library option located in the Bookplace tab, you can manage the addition and removal of your books. You can add and remove books as you wish to see the value of your books and effectively manage your holdings. On this page, you can see:

- The total number of your books
- The total value of your books
- The number of books you still have for sale
- As well as all the books you have saved

## How to see all your books?

To view your books in the Bookplace, go to the home page and click on "My Library."

In this Bookplace, you can see all the books you have saved. If the list is empty, it simply means that you haven't added any.

> If you have already entered books, but they are not appearing in your book space, [contact us](https://wa.me/+237698778055)

### *Accessing the Book List*

To view the list, simply click the "Open Inventory List" button on the book space homepage. From there, you can: [search for a book](#find-a-book-in-the-book-space), [edit a book](#edit-a-book), and [export your book list](#export-your-book-list).

## How to Add a Book to Your Library

You can save the books you own to know exactly what you have in terms of quantity and price.

You can then choose to give it to an individual, a loved one, or even a company, or to sell it.

To add a new book to your book space, simply go to the book space page or the dashboard and:

1. Click on "Add a new book"

2. Fill in the book information
3. Add a main image (required) and one or more secondary image(s) (optional)

4. Confirm to save using the ***Add*** button

> Note: If you feel the confirmation button isn't working, try scrolling back up the form and check that all the required fields (marked with a red *) are filled in.

## How to find a book in the book space?

In the book space, you can search for a book. You can use the search bar in the Book Space list or directly on the Book Space homepage.

### *Search by Keyword*

The search function allows you to find one of your books using a keyword. To perform a keyword search in the Book Space or from the Book Space list, you must:

1. Click on the Search bar.

2. Enter the word you are looking for.

3. The book will be displayed at the bottom.

### *Add a Filter*

Adding a filter allows you to search for one or more books with common attributes. Filters are located to the right of the search bar. You can apply one or more filters by selecting their values.

Note: If you cannot find your book, make sure you have entered it correctly or that you haven't already deleted it from the Book Space. If the book's name is correctly entered and it should be in the book, but you still can't find it, please contact [customer service](https://wa.me/+237698778055).

## How do I edit a book's information?

You can edit a book at any time after adding it to your book space, and it will be instantly updated in your book space and on Bookplace if you have already listed it for sale.

## How do I remove a book from my book space?

In your book space, hover over the book you want to remove. Click the three vertical dots, select the "Remove book" option, and then confirm the removal.

> Note: Removing a book is irreversible. Abelaa cannot recover books you have deleted. You can only recover deleted books by manually adding them back to your Bookspace. Please note that if the book was already listed in Bookplace, it will not be deleted if you have already sold or traded it.

## How to Export Your Book List

1. To export your Bookspace history from the Bookspace list:

2. Open the Bookspace list by clicking on ***Open Bookspace List***.

3. Click the Export button (you can choose between exporting to PDF, Excel, or CSV).

> Note:

> - If you want to export to multiple formats, you can. Simply press the corresponding buttons one after the other.
