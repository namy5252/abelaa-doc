# Inventory Management

Using the "Manage my inventory" option located in the Marketplace tab, you can manage the entry and exit of your items. You can add and empty it as needed to track the value of your items and effectively manage your inventory. On this page, you will see:

- The total number of your items
- The total value of your items
- The number of items you still have for sale
- As well as all the items you have saved

## How to view all your items?

To view your items in the inventory space, go to the homepage and click on "Manage my inventory".

In this inventory space, you can see all the items you have saved. If the list is empty, it simply means that you haven't added any items.

> If you have already entered items, but they do not appear in your inventory, [contact us](https://wa.me/+237698778055)

### *Access the list of items in stock*

To view the inventory in list format, simply click the "Open inventory list" button from the inventory homepage. From there, you can: [search for an item](#find-an-item-in-the-inventory), [edit an item](#edit-an-item), and [export your item list](#export-your-item-list).

## How to add an item to your inventory?

You can save the items you own to know exactly what you have in terms of quantity and price.

You can then choose to give it to an individual, a friend, or even a company, or to sell it.

To add a new item to your inventory, simply go to the inventory page or the dashboard and do the following:

1. Click on "Add a new item"
2. Enter the item information
3. Add a main image (required) and one or more secondary image(s) (optional)
4. Confirm to save using the ***Add*** button

> Note: If you feel the confirmation button isn't working, try scrolling back up the form and check if all the required fields (marked with a red *) are filled in.

## How to find an item in the inventory?

In the inventory, you can search for an item. You can use the search bar in the inventory list or directly on the inventory homepage.

### *Search by Keyword*

The search function allows you to find one of your items using a keyword. To perform a keyword search in the inventory or from the inventory list, you must:

1. Click on the Search bar.
2. Enter the word you are looking for.
3. The item will be displayed at the bottom.

### *Add a Filter*

Adding a filter allows you to search for one or more items with common attributes. Filters are located to the right of the search bar. You can apply one or more filters by selecting their values.

Note: If you cannot find your item, make sure you have entered it correctly or that you haven't already deleted it from the inventory. If the item name is incorrect, please contact customer service (https://wa.me/+237698778055).

## How do I edit an item's information?

You can edit an item at any time after adding it to your inventory. It will be instantly updated in your inventory and on the Marketplace if you have already listed it for sale.

## How do I delete an item from my inventory?

In your inventory, hover over the item you want to delete. Click the three vertical dots, select "Delete item," and then confirm the deletion.

> Note: Deleting an item is irreversible. Abelaa cannot recover items you have deleted. You can only recover deleted items by manually adding them back to your inventory. Please note that if the item was listed in the marketplace, it will not be deleted if you have already sold or traded it.

## How to export your item list

1. To export your inventory history from the inventory list:
2. Open the inventory list by clicking on ***Open inventory list***.
3. Click the Export button (you can choose between exporting to PDF, Excel, or CSV format).

> Note:

> - If you wish to export in multiple formats, it is possible. You simply need to press the corresponding buttons one after the other.
