# FAQ

## About Notifications

Notifications are only accessible on a device if the user has accepted them when opening the application. They are there to keep you informed of everything happening with your online products or orders.

### ***View Notifications***

Application notifications can be viewed in the top bar, next to the language selection option. Simply click on the small bell icon, and you will see all the notifications you have received. You can choose to:

- **Mark as Read**: By clicking on this option, you can mark all messages as already read.
